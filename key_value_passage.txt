Every key has a value, and every value has a key.
The dictionary holds the keys, and the keys hold the values.
Count the words, count the words, count the words again;
Counting is the work, and the work is never done.
Some words appear once, and some words appear often.
The, the, the: the most common word of all!
Capital or lowercase? The program cannot tell.
Python says Word and word are different, but we know they are the same.
So lower the case, strip the punctuation, and count again.
Is it Key, or key, or KEY? Count them all as one key.
